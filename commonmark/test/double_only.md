## Double-only formatting spec

```````````````````````````````` example
=a= **b**
.
<p>=a= <strong>b</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
==a=== **=b**
.
<p><strong>a</strong>= <strong>=b</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
===a== **b=**
.
<p>=<strong>a</strong> <strong>b=</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````
